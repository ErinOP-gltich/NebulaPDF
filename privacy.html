Your files never leave this tab.

Most online PDF tools upload your document to their server, work on it there and send it back. Nebula does the opposite: your browser downloads the tool code, and the work happens on your device.

Your files stay with you

Your browser opens, edits and saves every file itself. Nothing about your documents is sent anywhere, and closing the tab clears them from memory.

What your browser downloads

Nebula’s own code comes from this site. When a tool needs it, an open-source library loads from a public code host (cdnjs, jsDelivr or unpkg), fonts come from Google Fonts, and AI models from Hugging Face. Like any website, those services see your IP address and what they sent you, never your files.

AI tools

They run an open-source model on your device by default. A Claude option for sharper answers is coming soon. It will always be your choice: only then is a document’s text sent, to Anthropic, and Nebula itself won’t keep it.

No account, no tracking

There’s no sign-up, no advertising and no analytics. Like any website, the host (Vercel) keeps standard server logs of page requests.

Saved on your device

Your theme, motion setting and AI choice are remembered in your browser. If you save the tools for offline use, their code is kept in your browser’s cache. Clearing this site’s data removes all of it.

Check it yourself

Open your browser’s developer tools, watch the Network tab while you use a tool, and you’ll see no uploads. The code is open source on GitHub.
