A toolbox that respects your files.

Most online tools ask you to upload a file to someone else’s server just to merge two PDFs or shrink a photo. That’s a lot to ask for a contract, a payslip or a passport scan. Nebula does the same jobs inside your browser, so your files stay with you.

It’s free, there’s no account, and the code is open for anyone to read, check and improve. Made by Aman.

See the code on GitHub Report a problem

  • Private by design

    Your browser opens, edits and saves your files. There’s no server in the middle that could keep a copy.

  • Free, no sign-up

    No account, no email, no watermark stamped on your work.

  • Works offline

    Save the tools once and they keep working without a connection, in the browser or as an installed app.

  • Open source

    MIT-licensed. Read every line, run your own copy, or suggest a change.
