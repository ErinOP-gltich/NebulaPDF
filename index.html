Manifesto & Specs

Architecture

NebulaPDF was engineered to eliminate the compromise between convenience and security. Most web-based PDF converters upload your documents to remote servers, process them in a queue, and hold your data in temp folders.

By utilizing WebAssembly and modern browser APIs, we brought the entire print shop into your local environment. Data never leaves your machine.

Press Components

  • Canvas: HTML5 / Tailwind CSS
  • Engine: pdf-lib.js / jsPDF
  • Typesetter: Mammoth.js (DOCX)
  • Scanner: Tesseract.js (WASM OCR)
